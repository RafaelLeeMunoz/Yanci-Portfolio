About Me

I am a healthcare professional and Health Informatics specialist dedicated to transforming how health systems collect, interpret, and act on information. My career began at the bedside as an Outpatient Nurse at Xinhua Hospital, where I gained first-hand experience in the fast-paced realities of clinical documentation and patient care.

Driven by a desire to optimize these systems, I earned my Master's in Clinical Laboratory Science (Health Informatics) from the University of Massachusetts Lowell. This academic foundation allows me to bridge the gap between frontline providers and technology stakeholders, specializing in EHR optimization, interoperability standards like FHIR, and data management.

Beyond technical skills, I bring a unique perspective as a Bilingual Medical Interpreter. By navigating the linguistic and cultural complexities of patient care within Epic EHR environments, I ensure that health technology remains patient-centered and inclusive. I believe that thoughtful systems design and the intelligent use of data are the keys to reducing provider burnout and elevating the standard of global healthcare.

When I'm not analyzing data or clinical workflows, I find balance in arts and crafts — a creative practice that keeps my problem-solving mind curious, grounded, and focused on the beauty of handmade precision.
