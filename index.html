About Me

I am a healthcare professional with a Master's degree in Health Informatics, dedicated to transforming how health systems collect, interpret, and act on information to improve the lives of patients.

With experience spanning clinical operations, electronic health record systems, and data-driven quality improvement, I bridge the gap between patient care and health technology. My work is rooted in the belief that thoughtful systems design and the intelligent use of data can meaningfully improve both patient outcomes and provider workflows.

I bring a collaborative, cross-functional approach to every project — translating complex health data into clear, actionable insights for clinical teams, administrators, and technology stakeholders.

Outside of healthcare, I find balance and joy in arts and crafts — a creative outlet that keeps my problem-solving mind curious and grounded.
